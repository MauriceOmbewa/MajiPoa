Common questions

Do I need to return the empty jug?

For refills, yes — the rider swaps your empty jug for a full one. If you don't have a jug, order the new jug bundle once and use it from then on.

When am I charged for a recurring delivery?

On the morning of each delivery. You can skip or cancel any time before the charge goes out.

How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds go back to the M Pesa number that paid, usually within a few hours and always within 3 working days.

Can I order without an account?

Yes. A phone number is all we need. An account only saves your addresses, history and offers.
