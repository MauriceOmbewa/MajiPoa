You should know what's in your water.

Every vendor on MajiSafi submits their source, treatment process, permits and lab results before they can sell. We check them, publish what we verified, and pause vendors whose tests lapse.

What we check

  • Business registration and permits
  • Water source and abstraction licence
  • Treatment process and equipment
  • KEBS standardisation mark
  • Laboratory test results and date
  • Bottling and storage facility

What the numbers mean

pH 6.5 – 8.5
The acceptable range for drinking water in Kenya. Most purified water sits near 7.
TDS under 600 ppm
Total dissolved solids. Lower means fewer minerals and salts; RO water is usually under 100.
Test under 90 days old
We flag vendors whose latest lab result is older than this.

When something is wrong

Report a taste, smell or seal problem and we hold the vendor's batch while we look into it. You get a refund or replacement, and the result is recorded on the vendor's profile.
